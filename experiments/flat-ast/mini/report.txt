Method and limits
Inputs were read unchanged from the Octane worktree bench/mini directory.
Source SHA-256 hashes, script hashes, stage commits and binary hashes are recorded.
Each child ran serially with --script; stage order was rotated and reversed.
The injected mini driver executes Setup, starts Date.now, runs its fixed loop,
then stops the timer and invokes TearDown. Its timer therefore excludes initial
source parsing, Setup and TearDown; dynamic work inside b.run remains included.
Process wall time includes all of those phases, startup and teardown. The gap
between wall and reported time is NOT a pure compilation-time measurement.
RSS is per-child wait4 peak RSS, converted from bytes to MiB on macOS.
There was no CPU affinity/frequency lock or system-wide isolation. Date.now has
1 ms granularity, and individual component timings can be short. Do not interpret
small percentage changes or reversals between stages as causal improvements.
These fixed-iteration mini timings are not official Octane scores; no aggregate
score or confidence interval is reported.

Directional interpretation
* The broad mini workloads do not reproduce the large synthetic constant-heavy
  wins from the earlier targeted experiment. Most final-stage observations are
  close to baseline; this one round cannot establish a broad speedup.
* Mandreel is essentially flat: 1510/1505/1506/1509 reported ms at the four
  stages. Its RSS rises roughly 214.3 -> 224.5 MiB at the numeric stage and
  remains there. This is consistent with a temporary per-node analysis column;
  it is not an allocation-by-allocation attribution.
* TypeScript is also close: 1781/1768/1779/1785 ms; whole-process time stays
  around 1.85-1.87 s. No const-propagation benefit is established here.
* Zlib happens to show 9749 -> 9129 ms at the final stage (-6.4%). Treat this
  as an observation only. RegExp and GBEmu show large intermediate reversals
  (GBEmu 417/416/454/419 ms), illustrating why one sample is not proof.
* Existing embedded validation passed at each stage. There is no correctness
  failure to separate from performance in this selected round.
* Large retained heaps can mask transient compiler allocation differences in
  process peak RSS. A flat RSS result does not mean the analysis allocates nothing.
