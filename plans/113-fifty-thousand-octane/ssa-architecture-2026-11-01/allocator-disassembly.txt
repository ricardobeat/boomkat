only in complete-native totals, not in these loop ranges.

Example actual hot work: linear0x3e8 adds w0,w23,#1;0x3f4 stores x0 to a spill;
0x3f8 reloads it for the array index. CFG0x3e8 adds w4,w22,#1, sign extends
in x4, then moves x4 directly to x1 for the index. Other spills/reloads also
become register moves, so byte count reduction alone understates memory work.
