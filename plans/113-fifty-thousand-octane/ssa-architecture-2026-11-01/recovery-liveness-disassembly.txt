array values, positive valid integral indices, integer width/rowSize captures,
x/y within [0.5, bound], sufficient budget, and i remains <= width. This is
a static walk of an executable steady-state path, not a dynamic profile or
an average over clamp/final/guard-failure paths.

The out-of-line recovery region has 24 return-ending stubs in both captures.
Its static VM-slot stores fall 444 -> 327 and spill reloads 207 -> 94.
These counts sum emitted instructions across all stubs, not operations per
iteration: an exit executes only its selected stub. Pending CALL publication
falls from 14 -> 2 VM-slot stores and 10 -> 1 spill reload; this cost occurs
at the pending call, not each grid cell. The RET publication remains empty
apart from metadata/return because RETUNDEF has no semantic register inputs.
Thus 3456/3848 = 89.8% of total code-size savings are cold stub footprint;
the hot-path traffic reduction is independently established above. Code size
alone would overstate the reduction in repeatedly executed instructions.

METHOD / REPRODUCIBILITY
Lightweight text-only parser/walker: /tmp/bk-count-recovery-native.py.
Full selected spill-instruction listings: /tmp/bk-recovery-native-path-counts.txt.
The walker follows direct branches, successful in-body exact-conversion and
normal-number branches, unclamped coordinate branches, integer capture decode,
and the continuing inner backedge; it rejects any path entering cold stubs.
No engine process, benchmark, build, profiler, or source edit was run here.

ACTIVATION CHECK (pre-existing capture stdout logs)
Both captures report: isolated advect PASS; 40 SSA hits, 1 RET completion,
0 entry misses, 39 body handoffs, 7878 charged native edges, 10 budget exits.
Coverage therefore did not fall to produce the smaller native code. This
mechanism evidence is not a claim of an equivalent end-to-end speedup;
matched timing remains the performance gate.
